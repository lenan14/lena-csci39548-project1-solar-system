The Sun

The Sun is a star at the center of our solar system. It gives Earth light and heat, and it is made mostly of hydrogen and helium.

What Makes the Sun Glow?

Deep inside the Sun, hydrogen atoms combine to form helium. This releases energy that eventually reaches us as sunlight.

  • The Sun holds more than 99% of the solar system's mass.
  • Its gravity keeps all planets in orbit.
  • Its light helps warm Earth and support plant growth.
  • The visible layer is called the photosphere; the Sun has no solid surface.
