# Solar System Explorer

My small website about the solar system, made with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It does not use any frameworks or libraries!

### Open the Website

Open `webdev-day1.html` in a browser to see the homepage. Use the navigation bar to visit the Sun, Moon, and planet pages. Neptune was not in the rubric, but I added it anyway because it's still a planet!

### Pages and Interactions

- **Home** Start and stop the planet animation.
- **Sun** Show a clearly labeled solar-flare illustration (this is not a live alert by the way).
- **Moon** View a few Moon phases.
- **Earth** Switch a simple day/night lighting effect.
- **Mercury** Change the surface temperature.
- **Venus** Show or hide a fact about the surface.
- **Mars** Choose a surface feature to learn a fact.
- **Jupiter** Start a clearly labeled rotation illustration.
- **Saturn** Explore facts about its rings.
- **Uranus** Look at its seasons.
- **Neptune** Start and stop a clearly labeled rotation illustration.

The planet pages also have a weight calculator, at least five facts, and a NASA facts link as reference. Their colors and heading fonts vary by world while keeping a shared layout. The shared styles are in `styles.css`, and the page interactions are in `script.js`. The planet pictures are in `assests/`.

Each Sun, Moon, and planet page also includes a gallery of two NASA archive images with descriptive alt text and source credits.
